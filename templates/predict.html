How It Works

Our machine learning model analyzes multiple factors to predict a student's math score:

  • Demographic Factors: Gender, race/ethnicity, and parental education level
  • Economic Factors: Lunch type (indicator of socioeconomic status)
  • Preparation: Whether the student completed test preparation
  • Academic Performance: Reading and writing scores

The model was trained on 800 student records and uses ensemble methods (CatBoost + XGBoost) for accurate predictions.
